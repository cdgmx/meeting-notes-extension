How to enable access
  1. Switch to your meeting or video tab.
  2. Click Meeting Notes in Chrome’s toolbar or Extensions (puzzle piece) menu.
  3. Select Start listening here.

Repeat for each new tab. Chrome’s internal pages cannot be recorded.
